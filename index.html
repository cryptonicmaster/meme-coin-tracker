🔴 LIVE ON-CHAIN LAUNCHES

Solana / pump.fun tokens — surfaced once they show real early trading activity, not the instant they're minted

Extreme risk — read before using this section. The vast majority of pump.fun mints get zero real buyers and die within seconds, so each token is held quietly for about 90 seconds and only shown here if it clears a minimum bar of real activity (buyer count, volume, liquidity) — not just having been created. Clearing that bar is still not a safety or quality guarantee, just evidence some real trading happened. Each token is also cross-checked against RugCheck, a free Solana contract scanner — tokens explicitly flagged as already rugged, or with both mint and freeze authority still active, are filtered out automatically; a passing safety score only means those specific mechanisms are absent, not that the project is legitimate. This runs on unofficial third-party services (not pump.fun, DexScreener, or RugCheck's own apps) — any of them can go down or misbehave without notice.
